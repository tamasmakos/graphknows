# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phase 1: EU Parliament Focus (0-6 months)

- Coverage: EU Parliament debates, 'This is Europe' series, plenary sessions
- Target customers: Brussels early adopters -- EU affairs consultants, EP-adjacent think tanks, academic researchers
- Academic partnerships: ELTE (Budapest), LSE (London), College of Europe (Bruges)
- Goal: 10-20 paying users, strong qualitative feedback, RAGAS baseline established
- Key deliverable: working GraphKnows product with EP corpus fully ingested

## Phase 2: National Parliament Expansion (6-18 months)

- Bundestag first (t_e842525d scraper already planned) -- Germany is the largest EU member and has strong policy research infrastructure
- Then French Assemblee Nationale and UK Parliament (post-Brexit comparative analysis angle)
- Target: policy research firms, national affairs consultants, comparative politics academics
- Goal: 100+ paying users, first enterprise contracts
- Key deliverable: multi-parliament corpus with cross-parliament graph linkage

## Phase 3: Regulatory Bodies and Alerting (18-36 months)

- Expand to European Commission, European Central Bank, European Supervisory Authorities (ESAs)
- Launch monitoring/alerting product: real-time notifications when specific entities, topics, or relationships appear in new transcripts
- Target: compliance officers at multinationals, financial institutions, regulatory affairs teams
- Goal: first 1M EUR ARR, Series A readiness
- Key deliverable: real-time ingest pipeline + alerting API

## Partnership Opportunities

- Universities: research access agreements (data sharing for academic use in exchange for validation studies and co-authorship)
  - ELTE computational linguistics, LSE European politics, College of Europe EU studies
- Think tanks: Bruegel (economics + EU policy), ECFR (European Council on Foreign Relations)
  - Co-branded analysis, content marketing, user acquisition
- EU Institutions: European Parliament, European Commission (open data initiatives)
  - Data access, legitimacy, institutional sales pipeline

## Sales Channels

1. Direct outreach to EU affairs teams at lobbying firms and consultancies in Brussels
2. Inbound via political analysis blog and newsletter (content marketing: "what MEPs are saying about X")
3. API partnerships with media organizations (provide structured political data feeds)
4. Academic channel: conference presence (ECPR, NLP4PI workshops), open-access research tier

## Key Metric

Time-to-insight for a parliamentary brief: how long does it take a user to produce a structured brief on a topic using GraphGen vs. manual research? This is the primary value demonstration metric and should be benchmarked early against manual analyst workflows.
