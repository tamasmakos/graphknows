# Market Analysis

## Total Addressable Market

Political intelligence software is a growing segment of the broader GovTech and LegalTech markets. Global TAM is estimated at $2-3B, driven by increased regulatory complexity, geopolitical volatility, and the adoption of AI-native tools in policy and compliance workflows.

EU-focused SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market) is estimated at $200-400M, covering EU institutions, national governments, lobbying firms, think tanks, and multinationals operating in the EU regulatory environment.

## Demand Drivers

EU regulatory monitoring demand is accelerating due to:
- GDPR enforcement maturation and cross-border complexity
- AI Act implementation (2024-2026 rollout)
- Green Deal and ESG disclosure regulations
- Digital Markets Act and Digital Services Act compliance requirements
- Geopolitical instability driving need for political risk intelligence

## Key Buyer Personas

EU Affairs Manager (lobbying firm)
- Needs: track MEP positions, monitor debate trends, brief clients on legislative risks
- Pain: manually reading debate transcripts, fragmented monitoring tools

Policy Analyst (think tank)
- Needs: deep thematic analysis, entity relationship mapping, reproducible research
- Pain: no structured data layer on top of parliamentary text

Compliance Officer (multinational)
- Needs: early warning on regulatory shifts, audit trail for compliance decisions
- Pain: expensive enterprise tools with poor EU parliament coverage

Political Journalist
- Needs: influence mapping, "who said what when" traceability, cross-debate patterns
- Pain: no tool that connects discourse dots across time and speakers

## Pricing Benchmark

Professional political intelligence tools price at $100-500 per seat per month. Enterprise contracts are typically $15,000-100,000 per year depending on seats and data access depth. GraphGen's tiered model is positioned competitively within this range.

## Adjacent Markets

- Legal intelligence: contract AI, case law analysis (overlap on graph-structured document reasoning)
- Financial intelligence: earnings call analysis, central bank speech monitoring (same pipeline, different corpus)
- Risk and compliance: regulatory change monitoring, sanctions intelligence

These adjacent markets represent expansion opportunities post-PMF in the core political intelligence segment.
